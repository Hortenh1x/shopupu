### Implemented timers versus retention decisions

- Access JWTs: configured lifetime capped at 15 minutes. Refresh tokens: default 7 days, configurable within 1–90 days. These are validity limits, not immediate physical record deletion.
- Password-reset links: 30 minutes; email-verification links: 24 hours. MFA challenges: 5 minutes; privileged refresh assurance: original verification time plus 12 hours. Used recovery codes remain until replacement/erasure; no separate recovery-row retention timer was identified.
- [RefreshTokenCleanupJob](../src/main/java/com/example/shopupu/auth/service/RefreshTokenCleanupJob.java) runs cron `0 30 3 * * *`, with no explicit zone on the annotation. It deletes expired MFA challenges, and refresh/one-time rows whose expiration is more than one day old. Actual execution depends on scheduler/process availability and its configured/default timezone; there is no exact deletion-time guarantee.
- Account throttles, IP buckets, AI budgets and request caches are process-local. They have bounded windows/cache eviction, not durable investigative retention. See the identity/AI documents for their specific limits and single-instance assumptions.
- [Database recovery](database-recovery.md) proposes daily backups/RPO ≤24 hours and restore/RTO ≤60 minutes. These are unmeasured acceptance targets. Backup retention duration, storage location, encryption key owner and restore-drill evidence remain owner TODOs. Database dumps do not include uploaded images.
- No general scheduled purge for accounts, orders, audit rows, guest carts, retained payment/refund history, public uploads or backups was established by this bounded review. Choose periods and implement/verify them before claiming automatic deletion in public copy.
